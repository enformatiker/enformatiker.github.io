Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Pregnancy - Mom & Baby Tracker gives general information only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Follow your own care team and hospital instructions first. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

What the app is for

The app helps you keep a private record of dates, measurements you enter, logs and questions. It can show simple derived values such as pregnancy week, elapsed time or a commonly described contraction pattern. These are organisational helpers, not clinical measurements or predictions.

What the app is not for

  • It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
  • It does not replace a doctor, midwife, nurse or hospital service.
  • It does not monitor you, send alerts to clinicians or create a medical record.
  • It is not intended for emergencies or for making urgent health decisions.

Information you enter

The app can only work with what you type. Entries may be incomplete, out of date or inaccurate. A calculation shown by the app is not proof that something is normal or abnormal. If you are worried, contact your care team even if the app shows a reassuring value.

Sharing information

A doctor report PDF is created locally and shared only when you choose to do so. Review it before sending. The app does not send your entries to a clinic, hospital or third party automatically.

Urgent help

If you think something is wrong, or you need urgent advice, contact your local emergency number or the service your hospital gave you. Do not wait for an answer from the app or from support.

App support

For technical problems with the app, use the Support page or email enformatiker@gmail.com. Support cannot answer medical questions.
