Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Pregnancy - Mom & Baby Tracker mobile app handles information. Health entries stay on your device. Only Community posts you choose to publish are stored on our server; there is no account, no advertising tracking and no sale of personal information.

This policy covers the mobile app. GitHub Pages hosting logs are outside the app control.

1. Information you put into the app

You may choose to enter information such as:

  • Your name, an optional baby name, due date or last menstrual period date.
  • Hospital, doctor, blood type, height and pre-pregnancy weight.
  • Appointments, contraction and kick-session logs, sleep notes and doctor questions.
  • Birth preferences, preparation notes and Community posts you choose to publish.
  • Photos you select from your device library for the private photo diary.

Every field is optional or skip-able unless the app clearly says otherwise. You decide what to record.

2. How the information is collected and stored

Health entries are entered directly by you on your device and saved in the app local device storage. Photos are copied into app-private files on the same device. The app does not create an online account and does not upload your health entries to us. Community posts you publish are sent to our server so other app users can see them.

If you enable appointment reminders, the app asks for notification permission so it can show a local reminder. Notifications are configured without remote messaging. You can turn reminders off in the app or change the permission in your device settings.

3. How the information is used

Your entries are used only to provide app features, including:

  • Showing pregnancy dates, progress and tracked history.
  • Calculating durations and simple patterns from times you enter.
  • Creating a doctor report or birth-preference PDF when you choose to share one.
  • Creating a local JSON backup when you choose to export one.
  • Showing a local reminder when you have enabled one.
  • Publishing Community posts you choose to share and showing posts from other users.

We do not use your entries for advertising, profiling, analytics sent off the device, or automated decision-making.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or send your health information to third parties. When you tap a share action, the device share sheet opens and you choose the destination. When you open a legal, support or store link, the device browser or mail app handles it. Those actions happen only after you start them.

The app uses Expo and React Native device libraries for local storage, notifications, biometrics, photos, printing and sharing. These libraries provide device functions; the app does not transmit your entries to them as a service.

Community posts are stored in our Supabase database and are visible to other app users. Do not include your name, medical details or other private information in a Community post. Posts are refreshed about every 15 seconds while the app is open.

5. Retention and deletion

Your entries remain on the device until you delete them. You can delete individual items inside the app, use Profile → Reset App Data to clear all app data, or uninstall the app. Resetting app data also clears the accepted-terms record, so the first-launch agreement and onboarding appear again.

A JSON backup is a file you create and keep yourself. The app cannot delete copies you have saved elsewhere. Photos in the photo diary can be deleted in the app and are not included in the JSON backup. Uninstalling the app removes its app-local data.

6. Your choices and consent

  • Turn appointment reminders off in Profile → Notifications.
  • Revoke notification or photo access in your device settings.
  • Turn off App lock in Profile → App lock if you enabled it.
  • Export a backup at any time from Profile → Backup & restore.
  • Delete all local app data from Profile → Reset App Data.

Your health entries stay on the device, so no online request is needed to delete them. If you published a Community post and want it removed, delete it in the app; you can also ask support using the address below.

7. Children’s privacy

Pregnancy - Mom & Baby Tracker is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top will be updated, and the in-app agreement version will be bumped when the accepted terms change.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or about app data can be sent to enformatiker@gmail.com. For medical questions, please contact your own healthcare professional.
