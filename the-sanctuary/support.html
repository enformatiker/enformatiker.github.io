Feedback & Support

Last updated: 20 November 2026

For app questions, bug reports or feedback, email enformatiker@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as we can.

Support is for the app only and cannot give medical advice. For health questions, contact your own care team. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

What to include in a message

  • The app version shown in Profile → About.
  • Your device model and operating-system version.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • The steps to reproduce the issue, if there are any.
  • A screenshot if it helps. Please remove private or health details unless they are needed to understand a visual problem.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

App entries are stored locally on your device. There is no account and no health-data server for the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Use Profile → Reset App Data to clear all app data, or delete individual entries where the app offers a delete action. Uninstalling the app also removes its app-local data. Resetting clears the accepted-terms record, so onboarding starts again.

How do I keep a copy of my entries?

Open Profile → Backup & restore to export a JSON file. Store it in a private place: anyone with the file can read it. Photos from the photo diary are not part of that file.

Can I share a report with my doctor?

Yes. Profile → Doctor report creates a PDF locally and opens your device's share sheet. Review the PDF before sharing. The app does not send it anywhere by itself.

Where can I read the legal documents in my language?

Open Profile in the app for the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Medical Disclaimer in all supported app languages. The first-launch agreement also offers the full documents before you accept.

Feature requests

Suggestions are welcome at the same email address. Please describe the problem you want solved rather than a specific medical request, so we can keep the app general and non-diagnostic.
